Feature SummaryCivicLens ships two role-based experiences — a Citizen Dashboard and a Government Dashboard — connected by a shared AI grievance-processing pipeline. This document defines the functional requirements (FR), the complaint lifecycle, department routing rules, and the analytics required for launch.2. Citizen Dashboard2.1 Onboarding &amp; LocationIDRequirementPriorityFR-C01Citizen can register/login via Supabase Auth (email, phone OTP, or social login).MustFR-C02On first use, app requests device location permission; if granted, auto-detects city/area via Geolocation API.MustFR-C03If location permission is denied, citizen can manually select city and locality from a dropdown/search.MustFR-C04Citizen profile stores preferred language for future sessions.Should2.2 Complaint SubmissionIDRequirementPriorityFR-C05Citizen can submit a complaint via text, voice recording, or one or more images (or any combination).MustFR-C06Voice and text input support multiple Indian languages; system transcribes/translates for AI processing.MustFR-C07Image upload supports computer-vision analysis to help classify the issue type (e.g., pothole, garbage pile, waterlogging).MustFR-C08Complaint location is auto-tagged from device GPS, with an editable pin on a map (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap) before submission.MustFR-C09System runs duplicate-complaint detection (location proximity + content/image similarity) and, if a likely duplicate is found, prompts the citizen to confirm/merge instead of creating a new record.MustFR-C10On submission, AI assigns a preliminary category, department, and priority level; citizen receives a confirmation with a tracking ID.Must2.3 Tracking &amp; NotificationsIDRequirementPriorityFR-C11Citizen can view real-time status of each submitted complaint: Submitted → Accepted/Rejected → In Progress → Resolved.MustFR-C12Citizen receives the department-provided estimated resolution time once a complaint is accepted.MustFR-C13Citizen receives notifications (in-app, and email/SMS if configured) on every status change.ShouldFR-C14Citizen can view a history/list of all their past and active complaints.MustFR-C15Citizen can view resolution proof (photo/notes) uploaded by the department upon closure.Should3. Government Dashboard3.1 Complaint TriageIDRequirementPriorityFR-G01Officer sees an incoming queue of complaints routed to their department, sorted by AI-assigned priority.MustFR-G02Officer can accept a complaint and provide/adjust the estimated resolution time.MustFR-G03Officer can reject a complaint with a required reason (e.g., duplicate, out of jurisdiction, invalid).MustFR-G04Officer can reassign a complaint to a different department or sub-branch if mis-routed.ShouldFR-G05Officer can update complaint status (In Progress, Resolved) and attach resolution notes/photos.MustFR-G06System flags critical complaints (public-health/safety risk, high population-density area) at the top of every queue.Must3.2 AI Grievance CopilotIDRequirementPriorityFR-G07For every complaint, the Copilot suggests: category, target department/sub-branch, priority score, and rationale.MustFR-G08Priority scoring factors in issue severity, population density of the reported area, and complaint recurrence in that location.MustFR-G09Officer can accept or override any AI suggestion; overrides are logged to improve future model routing.ShouldFR-G10Copilot surfaces related/duplicate open complaints near the same location when an officer opens a complaint.Should3.3 Administrator AnalyticsIDRequirementPriorityFR-A01Complaints by category — volume and trend over time, filterable by date range.MustFR-A02Complaints by location — map/heatmap view of complaint density across Indore.MustFR-A03Department workload — open/in-progress/resolved counts per department and sub-branch.MustFR-A04Resolution time — average and median time-to-resolution per department and category.MustFR-A05Critical complaints — live count and list of AI-flagged critical/high-priority open complaints.MustFR-A06Pending complaints — count and aging breakdown (e.g., 0–2 days, 3–7 days, 7+ days).MustFR-A07SLA violations — count of complaints that exceeded their department SLA target, filterable by department.MustFR-A08Admin can export any analytics view as CSV/PDF for reporting.Could4. Complaint Lifecycle1. Citizen registers/logs in and selects or auto-detects location.2. Citizen submits a complaint (voice, image, and/or text) — location is tagged on the map.3. AI pipeline runs: transcription/translation (if voice), image classification (if photo present), duplicate detection, category + department classification, and priority scoring.4. If a duplicate is detected above the confidence threshold, the citizen is prompted to link to the existing complaint instead of creating a new one.5. Complaint enters the relevant department queue in the Government Dashboard, ranked by priority.6. Officer reviews the AI suggestion, accepts or rejects the complaint, and (if accepted) sets/confirms an estimated resolution time.7. Citizen is notified of acceptance/rejection and the estimated resolution time.8. Officer updates status as work progresses (In Progress → Resolved), optionally attaching resolution photos/notes.9. Citizen is notified at each status change and can view the final resolution record.10. Resolved and rejected complaints feed into analytics (resolution time, SLA compliance, category/location trends).5.
